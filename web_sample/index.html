ABOUT OUR COMPANY

Integrated Solutions for Construction & Industry

PT Nattu Global Synergy

PT Nattu Global Synergy is an integrated general construction and wholesale trading enterprise established to deliver high-quality construction standards and operational efficiency for commercial, industrial, and public sector clients.

Strategically located in Central Jakarta, we combine engineering excellence in building construction with a nationwide supply network for metal construction materials and precision electrical components.

To become a leading and trusted general construction partner, metal material provider, and electronic spare parts supplier in Indonesia, committed to quality, competitiveness, and sustainable development.

  • Deliver premium office building construction and renovation services.
  • Ensure continuous supply of precision structural metal materials.
  • Supply reliable electronic spare parts and automation devices.
  • Prioritize Health, Safety & Environment (HSE/K3) standards.
